Resus Bay 2: The Full Hospital

Night shift. You run the emergency department, and the hospital is nearly full. Triage every arrival, treat the sick, wheel stretchers to the right unit, and keep the department from gridlocking.

  • Move: W A S D, Shift sprint, Space jump, mouse look.
  • Triage: ambulance patients arrive on stretchers at the entrance; walk-ins sit in the waiting room. Examine them (E), take triage vitals and assign an ESI level 1–5. Under-triage a heart attack and they may die in a chair.
  • Stretchers: look at one and press F to push it, F again to park. Parked near a bay it snaps into place with brakes on. Moving a patient disconnects the monitor and ventilator.
  • Rooms: 4 resus bays (monitor, ventilator, curtains), hallway spaces (no monitor), ICU, ORs, CT, and wards in three wings (5 beds per ward). Click monitors to change pages, look at a ventilator: click = FiO₂, R = rate, T = tidal volume, Y = PEEP, click curtains to open or close.
  • Disposition: once stable, a patient needs a unit (ICU, ward, OR) or discharge. If the unit is full they board in your ED, bays stay blocked, ambulances ramp outside and people wait hours. Use the Bed Board at the nurse station.
    Going home? Write a discharge prescription first (patient panel, Disposition, or the Rx terminal in the Pharmacy).
  • Kit: Supply Room (east of the lobby), Crash Cart (corridor). Wheeled medication, crash and equipment carts sit in the ORs, the ED hallway and the corridors by the ICU and wards: look at one, press E to take supplies, F to push it anywhere and F again to park. Hotbar holds 9 stacks.

Educational game with simplified physiology. Not a clinical training tool. Needs a desktop browser with keyboard and mouse. Clock runs 1 s = 1 minute for waits; critical patients are time-compressed.
