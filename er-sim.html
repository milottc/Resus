Resus Bay

You are the emergency physician on a night shift. Ambulances keep arriving. Walk the department, fetch equipment, examine patients and treat them before they die. Three deaths ends your shift.

  • Move: W A S D, Shift to sprint, Space to jump, mouse to look.
  • Examine: look at a bed and press E. Check A-B-C-D-E, attach the monitor, then treat.
  • Equipment: drugs and kit live in the Supply Room (east, south side) and the Crash Cart in the corridor. Your hotbar holds 9 stacks, so choose what to carry.
  • Use an item: select it (1–9), aim at the patient and click. Anything you can't do by click is in the examine panel.
  • Compressions and bag-mask only continue while you stay next to the bed.

Educational game with simplified physiology. Not a clinical training tool. Needs a desktop browser with keyboard and mouse.
